# MYT-004 Verify Plasma decorations in toolkit candidates

## Description

Verify NFR-3 before choosing the toolkit. In Plasma, add, remove, and reorder title-bar buttons across both sides. Verify that the candidate window reflects the configured layout and that Keep Above Others works. If custom integration is needed, prototype it before committing to the toolkit.

## Acceptance criteria

- Confirm the candidate respects additions, removals, and reordering on both sides and that Keep Above Others works. Demonstrate any required custom integration.
